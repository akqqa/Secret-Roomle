Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Secret Roomle ("we," "us," or "the site") handles information when you visit https://roomle.net.

What We Collect

Secret Roomle does not have a server, database, or user accounts. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information directly. There are no sign-ups, no forms, and no way for us to identify individual visitors.

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display ads on this site. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites, in order to provide ads about goods and services that may interest you.

This may include information such as your approximate location, device type, and inferred characteristics (like age range or interests) used for ad personalization. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data here: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ads Settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out: optout.aboutads.info

Analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall site traffic, such as how many people visit and which pages are viewed. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not track or collect personal data that could identify you individually. It is designed to be privacy-friendly and does not use any client-side state (like cookies or local storage) to track visitors across sites.

You can learn more about Cloudflare's approach to privacy-focused analytics here: cloudflare.com/web-analytics

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. Cookies present on this site are set by Google AdSense for ad delivery and personalization purposes, as described above. Our analytics tool (Cloudflare Web Analytics) does not use cookies at all.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Google's advertising services are configured to comply with applicable regulations regarding ads shown to children.

Your Rights (EU/UK/California Visitors)

If you are located in the EU, UK, or California, you may have rights regarding data collected by third parties like Google, including the right to access, delete, or opt out of the sale/sharing of your data. These requests should be directed to Google, as we do not hold any of your data ourselves. See Google's policy link above for how to exercise these rights.
